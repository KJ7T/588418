## Recording, logging, or monitoring

This node does not record or log audio. I use it as my main AllStar node when I am operating in Grapeview, Washington. 

It is connected to my SIP phone using a private node number, allowing me to quickly disconnect the phone if needed.

## Restrictions or usage policy

This is a publicly accessible node.
